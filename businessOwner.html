Could your business get help towards the cost of solar?

Explore government-backed support and incentives.

Small businesses may be able to benefit from government-backed schemes, local funding, tax allowances and payments for renewable electricity exported to the grid.

Smart Export Guarantee (SEG)

The Smart Export Guarantee allows eligible businesses with solar panels to receive payments from participating electricity suppliers for renewable electricity exported to the grid.

Local and regional funding

Some London boroughs and local authorities periodically offer energy-efficiency or decarbonisation grants for small businesses. Availability and eligibility vary by scheme.

Capital allowances

Businesses may also be able to claim relevant capital allowances against qualifying expenditure. Businesses should obtain professional tax advice before relying on any allowance.
